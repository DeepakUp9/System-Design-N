Question:
 What challenges might arise when implementing deep vs shallow cloning in the Prototype Pattern? 
 How would you address them in real-world scenarios?
Example Use-Case:
 You have a CustomerOrder object that contains a list of product references and nested shipping address. 
 If you clone it for duplication, and only shallow copy is used, 
 modifications to address or product list might affect the original. 
 Deep cloning is necessary here to avoid unintended side-effects.
