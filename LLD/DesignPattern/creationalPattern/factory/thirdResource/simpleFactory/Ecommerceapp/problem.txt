Question:
 In what scenarios would you prefer using a Simple Factory over directly 
 instantiating objects with constructors? Provide a real-world example.
Example:
 In an e-commerce app, when placing an order, the payment method varies (credit card, PayPal, wallet). 
 Instead of clients creating payment objects directly, a Simple Factory handles object creation based on the payment method selected by the user, 
 making the client code cleaner and more maintainable.
