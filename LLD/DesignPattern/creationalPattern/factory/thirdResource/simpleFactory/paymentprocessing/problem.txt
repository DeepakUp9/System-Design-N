Question:
 Imagine you are building a payment processing system supporting multiple payment types (credit card, UPI, wallet). 
 How would you design a Simple Factory to handle the creation of different payment processors? How would you manage adding new payment types later without modifying the factory class?
Example:
 The Simple Factory receives a payment type identifier and returns the appropriate payment processor instance. 
 To handle adding new payment types without modifying the factory, 
 you might integrate a registry or configuration-based factory to register new payment processors dynamically.
