What are the limitations of the Simple Factory Pattern, and how can these limitations 
impact maintainability and scalability in large projects?
Example:
 Suppose you keep adding new vehicle types (Car, Bike, Truck) to a Vehicle Factory. 
 The factory class grows huge and harder to maintain, 
 violating the Open/Closed Principle because you have to modify the factory every time you add a new type. 
 This makes scaling the system difficult.
